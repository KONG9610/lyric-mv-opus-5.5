## How it was made · 制作过程

One conversation, start to finish: questions first, a storyboard, model sheets for the two band characters drawn from
the band's reference sheets, a first minute, then the whole song, then four rounds of revisions (a rocket instead of a
shadow, a ticket swallowed instead of a stamp on the forehead, a hand-lettered title, heavier and then lighter type).
The pitfalls list in the skill is what that conversation learned.

The second film took one long conversation too. It began with a reference video whose look the person liked, became a
story about a language model after they said "the lead is really an AI", and changed most when they offered a picture of
a human "Claude" and proposed generating a set of expressions in one image. From there Claude asked for art in
batches, and the person became, in their words, its image-generation agent. Five full cuts; the ending was rewritten
three times.
